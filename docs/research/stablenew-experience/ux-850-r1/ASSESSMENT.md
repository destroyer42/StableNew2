UX-DEC-072 should become a methodology specification, not another broad preference questionnaire. [NIST randomized block designs](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/pri/section3/pri332.htm).

A small controlled cohort can satisfy an existing field-publication contract without proving universal model superiority. Use qualitative limitations when quantitative uncertainty is unsupported. Repeated imports and repeated ratings of one artifact are not independent replications. Human preference and technical measurements may legitimately disagree.
