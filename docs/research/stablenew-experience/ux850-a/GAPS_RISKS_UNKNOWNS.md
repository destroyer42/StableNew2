## Product-use dimensions beyond the decision matrix

The requested six dimensions (implementation, GUI, executed correctness, installed readiness, qualification, payoff) are present for every decision. The framework also asks grounded usage frequency and sustainable maintenance/inference cost: **both are unmeasured for every journey**. No local operator DB was mined and no nominal population/percentage/ROI is invented. Manual prompting/masking, explicit model/profile choice, bounded artifact browsing, human ratings and ordered clip assembly are existing alternatives while advanced AI/campaign features are unavailable.

## Assessment-framework critique

The earlier coarse matrix mixed invariant constraints with conflicting product requirements and under-credited scanning/encode profiles. The revised method requires a concrete contradiction before “conflicting”, a bounded contract plus negative search before “missing”, and a source binding before “reachable”. It adds separate all-row readiness/qualification/payoff columns and preserves partial control coverage. Remaining blind spots are same-author confirmation bias, absence of operator denominators and unexecuted adversarial controls. A truly independent reviewer and authorized operator task study could change priority; they are not fabricated as completed review.
