History, Review, Photo results, Motion Recent Outputs and Learning grids provide overlapping artifact access. The design gap is one understandable cross-source journey, not absence of every gallery, picker or comparison function. Navigation and content-visibility policies must be retained by any future unified projection. Keyboard/short-window/DPI accessibility remains unverified; a statically bound action can still be visually hard to reach.
