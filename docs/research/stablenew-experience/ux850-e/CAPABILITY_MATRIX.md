# Capability feasibility matrix

Classifications describe the **bounded capability named in each row**, at E's exact base. They are analytical judgments, not job states, qualification statuses or permissions. **existing** means source mechanism with the stated limits; **feasible bounded extension** means an understood service/projection extension subject to its own authorized package; **dependent on qualification** means physical/safety/quality evidence is essential before consequential use; **experimental** means scientific/product validity is unestablished; **blocked** means a named standing policy/authority or missing contract prevents the described enablement. Categories are intentionally not a linear maturity score.

All hardware, latency, throughput, installed availability and headroom here are **unmeasured**. Catalog support and model file sizes do not establish those facts. Source anchors E01–E48 are defined in [SOURCE_INDEX.md](SOURCE_INDEX.md). Gates G1–G8 are the retained [D research gates](../ux850-d/EXTENSION_SEAMS_GATES.md), not new owner decisions.

The matrix separates available mechanisms from broader owner outcomes. ## Reachability and product-evidence boundary

The six-factor assessment is deliberately uneven: code/API existence is anchored above, static route evidence is below, and correctness is limited to inspected contracts/accepted unchanged evidence. Task usefulness is the hypothesis and manual alternative in each row. **Usage frequency/denominator and sustainable maintenance/inference cost are unmeasured for every capability**, including existing ones; they are not zero. No route below was exercised in Tk in E.
