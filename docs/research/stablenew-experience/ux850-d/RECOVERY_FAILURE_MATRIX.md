## Failure classification discipline

Known **absent before transaction**, **committed with lost acknowledgement**, and **unknown/unreadable** are different outcomes. The last two cannot authorize automatic retry. Existing strict identity/fingerprint checks are the starting point for future recovery. Research does not change terminal encodings, add a schema state, alter cancel semantics or choose unresolved product behavior.

No live incident frequency, resource threshold, throughput, operator success rate, media quality, backend cancellation or power-loss durability was measured. Existing test source demonstrates intended deterministic assertions only; accepted historical execution evidence stays attached to its original identity and scope.
